#BC Social

BC Social is a social platform designed specifically for the Boston College community. It brings campus connections, clubs, communities, events, messaging, news, and a student marketplace into one centralized platform.

Instead of relying on separate apps for different parts of campus life, BC Social gives students one place to discover what is happening around campus and connect with other verified BC students.

Features
BC-only authentication — Sign in using a verified bc.edu Google account.
Student Profiles — Add your class year, majors, bio, interests, and club involvement.
Home Feed — Discover events, club activity, marketplace listings, and other campus updates.
Connections — Send, accept, decline, and manage connections with other BC students.
Messaging — Individual DMs, club group chats, and dedicated marketplace conversations.
Events — Create events, discover events around campus, and RSVP.
Clubs & Communities — Discover official BC organizations and student-created interest communities.
Marketplace — Buy and sell items within the verified BC community.
News — Browse BC-specific and general news with search and topic filters.
Marketplace

The Marketplace provides a BC-focused alternative to general online marketplaces. Students can search and filter listings, view detailed item information, and contact sellers directly. Sellers can create listings with a title, price, category, description, and photo and mark items as sold when they are no longer available. Marketplace conversations are kept separate from regular direct messages so students can contact sellers without first becoming connections.
